About PinClip

PinClip is a free web tool that takes a public Pinterest video URL and gives you back the MP4. That is the whole product. This page explains who runs it, how it actually works, and how it pays for itself.

Who we are

PinClip is run by two people in India — one writing the code, one writing the guides — as a side project, not a company. There is no team, no office and no investor. Everything published here is written by us; we do not commission or generate filler articles to chase search traffic.

When something on this site says “we measured”, it means we actually ran the measurement and published the data. The resolution study is the clearest example: 23 pins, downloaded and probed with ffmpeg, with the raw CSV attached so anyone can check the numbers.

You can reach us at orderbusinesspromotion@gmail.com. It is a real inbox and we answer it.

How it works

When you paste a link, a small Python service resolves the pin and reads the list of media renditions Pinterest publishes for it. Pinterest serves most videos two ways at once: an HLS ladder of video-only rungs with a separate audio stream, and one or more self-contained progressive MP4s. The service picks the largest rendition, uses ffmpeg to mux audio and video back together if the chosen rung needs it, and streams the result to your browser.

Nothing is kept. The URL you paste is held in memory for the length of the request and never written to a database. The video file is streamed through and discarded — there is no media library on our servers, which is also why we cannot offer download history.

What we will not claim

This category is full of sites promising 4K, guaranteed Full HD and watermark removal. We measured what Pinterest actually serves and none of that held up, so we do not say it.

  • We do not promise 1080p. In our sample nothing exceeded 720×1280, and five pins advertised a resolution higher than the file they delivered.
  • “No watermark” here means only that we add none. Marks a creator burned into the video before uploading cannot be removed by any tool.
  • We do not claim to be the fastest or most reliable option, because we have no way to measure that against every competitor and neither does anyone else making the claim.

The full list of things the tool cannot do is on the limitations page. We would rather you read it before you need it.

Where we stand on copyright

Every video PinClip touches belongs to whoever made it. We claim nothing, host nothing and store nothing. Saving a public video for your own offline use is generally fine; republishing it, monetising it, or passing it off as your own is not, and no wording on this site changes that.

We act on valid takedown notices and can block specific pins, boards or accounts from being processed — see the DMCA policy. If you are a creator with a concern, email us directly; you do not need to file a formal notice to start a conversation.

How this is funded

Advertising, through Google AdSense. That is the only revenue. There is no paid tier, no affiliate links in the articles, and nothing is sold. Ads are also why the site asks about cookies: until you choose, advertising and analytics storage default to denied, and declining costs you no functionality. Details are in the cookie policy.

The trade-off is worth stating plainly: the site exists because ads pay for the server, and the content is written to be worth reading rather than to fill space around them.
