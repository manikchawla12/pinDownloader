Everything You Need to Know About Pinterest Reels

Pinterest has heavily invested in short-form video content over the past few years, launching features like Idea Pins and more recently Pinterest Reels. These vertical, short-form videos are designed for quick consumption and are packed with condensed, actionable content — from 30-second makeup tutorials and rapid recipe demonstrations to fashion styling tips and home organization hacks.

Unlike standard Pinterest video pins, Reels and Idea Pins are specifically optimized for mobile viewing and are often shot in vertical (9:16) format. They've become one of the fastest-growing content types on the platform, with Pinterest reporting that engagement on short-form video content has increased significantly throughout 2025 and into 2026.

Reels vs. Idea Pins — What's the Difference?

Idea Pins were Pinterest's initial foray into stories-like content. They can contain multiple pages with a mix of video clips, images, text overlays, and interactive elements. Originally, Idea Pins couldn't receive outbound links, making them a pure engagement format.

Pinterest Reels represent the platform's newer approach to short-form video, more closely aligned with Instagram Reels and TikTok's format. They typically feature a single video clip with engaging transitions, music, and text overlays.

Reels & Idea Pins: Common Questions

What is the difference between an Idea Pin and a Reel?

An Idea Pin is a multi-page format: it can hold up to twenty pages of video, images and text that a viewer swipes through, and it stays permanently on the creator's profile. A Reel is a single short vertical clip designed for the feed. Because an Idea Pin can hold several video pages, our tool extracts the video content it finds — for multi-page pins you may need to save pages individually.

What resolution are Pinterest Reels?

Almost always 1080 x 1920 (9:16 vertical) at 30 fps. Pinterest recommends creators upload at that size, so it is what the tool returns in the large majority of cases. Some older Idea Pins were uploaded at 720 x 1280 and will download at that size.

Will the creator's text overlays and stickers be in the downloaded file?

It depends on how they were added. Text burned into the video before upload is part of the pixels and will be in your file. Text and stickers added inside Pinterest's own editor are rendered by the app on top of the video, so they are not in the underlying file and will not appear in your download.

Can I download a Reel that has music on it?

If the audio is part of the uploaded video file, it comes with the download. If Pinterest added a licensed track through its music library, that audio is mixed at playback time and may not be present. Bear in mind that licensed music carries its own copyright, entirely separate from the video.

Why did only part of an Idea Pin download?

Multi-page Idea Pins are technically several separate media items behind one URL. The tool resolves the primary video for the pin. To collect every page, open each page individually in the Pinterest web interface and copy that page's own link.
